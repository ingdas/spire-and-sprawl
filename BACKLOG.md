# Gameplay backlog

Ordered by expected impact on gameplay depth: how many new decisions an item creates per run, and how much it changes which builds can win. Engagement-only features such as dailies and streaks sit at the bottom on purpose.

Status: ✅ done · 🔨 in progress · blank = not started

| # | Item | Why it matters | Status |
|---|------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | **Biome acts.** A run is 3 acts of 4 nights. After nights 4 and 8 the island is carried to a new biome with its own rules: Meadow, then 2 of Dunes, Glacier and Caldera. | Each run gets a mid-run strategy pivot, and a build that wins one act can fail the next. | ✅ |
| 2 | **The journey costs something.** In flight, blocks on the island's rim break off and towers above 10 lose their tops. | Before the trip, both wide and tall builds have to weigh what they'll lose. | ✅ |
| 3 | **Unique blocks.** Rare one-of-a-kind blocks to build around: Anvil, Bell, Powder Keg, Omen Stone, Fountain, Mirror. One is guaranteed in the free draft once per act. | These are build-defining pieces, the closest thing to legendary items in a roguelite. | ✅ |
| 4 | **Material-specific topple landings.** When a tower is pushed over, ember splashes fire, crystal shatters into shards, ward leaves a slow field and garden scatters gold. | What a tower is made of now matters when it's knocked down, not only while it stands. | ✅ |
| 5 | **Counter-enemies.** Climber (walks over walls, drawn by wide builds), Bomber (a flier that bombs the top 2 blocks off the tallest tower, drawn by tall builds), Bulwark (turrets do half damage, while crushes, blasts and the core do full; drawn by turret-heavy builds). | Each one punishes a single dominant line, which forces mixed builds. | ✅ |
| 6 | **Biome terrain.** Rock outcrops are placed on arrival in a new biome, plus 3 erupting lava vents in the Caldera. | Every map has its own geometry to build around. | ✅ |
| 7 | **Night contracts.** At dawn, choose a normal night or one of 2 contracts: Quiet night, Bounty hunt (+50% enemies for +10 gold), Sky raid (fliers for a free relic) or Siege (brutes and bulwarks for +1 topple and +6 gold). | Lets you steer risk and reward instead of only reacting. | ✅ |
| 8 | **A different boss per act.** Act guardians on nights 4 and 8: Burrowing Wyrm (tunnels under walls and can only be hit while surfaced) and Sky Leviathan (rips the tops off towers). | Climaxes that test different builds. | ✅ |
| 9 | **Demolish during the day.** Remove up to 2 of your own blocks per day; the blocks above fall into the gap. | Lets you reshape your build, set up topples and remove mistakes. | ✅ |
| 10 | **Material adjacency reactions.** For example, ember next to garden makes smoke (slows), crystal next to ward makes frost beams. | Gives a placement puzzle in 3D, beyond simple stacking. | ✅ |
| 11 | **Forge at dawn.** Change one block of a piece to another material, add a block, or make it golden (+HP). | Gives deckbuilding-style improvement of your bag. | ✅ |
| 12 | **Weather per night.** Fog cuts range, storm lightning hits the tallest tower, and a full moon makes enemies faster but pays more. | Each night becomes a small puzzle that is telegraphed during the day. | ✅ |
| 13 | **Reserve slot.** Hold one piece over to the next day. | Lets you plan combos across days. | ✅ |
| 14 | **Chain collapses.** A toppled tower that hits another tower can knock it over too. | Rewards domino layouts. | ✅ |
| 15 | **Elite affixes.** Elites roll traits such as Armored, Regenerating, Splitting or Hasted. | Elite nights stop feeling the same each run. | ✅ |
| 16 | **Heat levels.** Pick difficulty modifiers after your first win. | Long-term mastery depth. | ✅ |
| 17 | **Interest economy.** +1 gold at dawn for every 10 gold saved, up to a cap. | Creates a spend-now-or-save tension. | ✅ |
| 18 | **Emergency build.** Spend gold to drop a single stone block mid-night. | Gives you something to do during tense nights. | ✅ |
| 19 | **Core upgrades.** Spend gold to grow the core's pulse radius or damage. | Opens a "turtle around the core" strategy. | ✅ |
| 20 | **Turret targeting.** A single Aim setting for all turrets, switchable mid-night: First, Strongest or Fliers. | More control over a tall build's turrets. | ✅ |
| 21 | **Call the night early.** Skip the remaining placement for bonus gold. | Lets you trade tempo for economy. | ✅ |
| 22 | **Relic synergies.** Domino Theory (endless chain topples), Storm Caller (every night is stormy, crystal lightning ×2) and Tipping Point (+1 topple, and topples pay gold). | Rewards discovering combos. | ✅ |
| 23 | **Dawn events.** On about a third of mornings: a merchant (unique block for 6 gold), a shrine (3 core HP for a relic), a gambler or a quarry (a free rock pillar). | Adds variety and risky choices. | ✅ |
| 24 | **More founders.** Quarryman (stone, rock and an Anvil; unlocked by Avalanche) and Pyromancer (embers and a Powder Keg; unlocked by First Light). | Different opening strategies. | ✅ |
| 25 | **Piece rarity.** 15% of draft offers come gilded (+50% HP), alongside the once-per-act unique blocks. | Clearer draft decisions. | ✅ |
| 26 | **Island props.** Biome rock outcrops can be built on and toppled like towers. | Extra tactical tools from the map. | ✅ |
| 27 | **Block repair (replaces weathering).** A "Patch walls" service at dawn repairs every damaged block for 4 gold. Damage used to be permanent, so this fills a real gap; extra upkeep pressure would be a poor fit for a young audience. | Upkeep pressure on big sprawls. | ✅ |
| 28 | **Second core in act 3.** Defend two points. | Changes the shape of the late game. | |
| 29 | **New challenges for the new systems.** Domino (a 3-tower chain), Globetrotter (survive a Caldera night) and Collector (3 unique blocks at once). Each unlocks one of the synergy relics. | Points players at the new mechanics. | ✅ |
| 30 | **Daily seed.** | An engagement feature, so lowest priority. | |
